## 0. Key findings in one page

**Sweden.** The rules in force are **NFS 2023:8** (Naturvårdsverkets föreskrifter om vapen, vapentillbehör och ammunition för jakt), in force since **1 Aug 2023**. NFS 2023:7 repealed §§ 2–22 of NFS 2002:18, *together with their allmänna råd*. Naturvårdsverket's page lists no amendments to NFS 2023:8 as of Nov 2026. The rules:

- **Gauges:** 12, 16, 20, 24, 28, 32 or .410.
- **Pellet size:** at most **4.1 mm (US 1)**. There is **no legal minimum pellet size and no legal maximum range** for any species.
- **Species:** 12/16/20 gauge may be used on everything except älg, hjortar, visent, bison, myskoxe, mufflonfår, varg, björn, sälar, vildsvin, järv and bäver. So **rådjur, räv, hare, gäss and lodjur may be shot with shot**. The small gauges (24/28/32/.410) are limited to small game and listed birds.
- **Slugs:** a single-barrel 12/16/20 gauge fitted with sights may fire slugs at vildsvin, dovhjort and mufflonfår (shooter qualified for klass 1) and at **rådjur** (shooter qualified for klass 2). Slugs are not allowed on älg or kronhjort. There is no slug energy rule.
- **Distances:** the old advisory limits (rådjur and gäss ≤ 20 m with shot, slug ≤ 40 m) were *repealed* with NFS 2002:18.
- **Jägarexamen:** in the distance-judging test, targets at ≤ 20 m are "inom hagelhåll" (within shotgun range) and targets at 30 m are not. A hit on the running target at 20 m needs ≥ 8 pellets in a Ø 9 cm circle (hare/fox) or ≥ 28 in a Ø 18 cm circle (roe deer) (NFS 2015:10, 17 § and 20 §).

**Finland.** The rules are in Metsästysasetus 666/1993 (latest amendment 30.12.2025/1432):

- **Gauge:** 10 to 20 for game. Gauges below 20 (down to 36/.410) only for listed small species (17 §).
- **Species:** shot is banned for hirvi, valkohäntäpeura, saksanhirvi, metsäpeura, kuusipeura, japaninpeura, karhu, villisika, hylje and mufloni (18 §). **Metsäkauris (roe deer) may be shot with shot.**
- **Slugs:** banned only for **hirvi and karhu** (18 §).
- **No pellet-size, energy or range rule for shot** appears in the decree.
- **Lead:** banned for **waterfowl hunting** since 1996 (Metsästyslaki 615/1993, 33 §).
- **Guidance (Riistakeskus):** roe deer with shot **≤ 25 m with 3.5–4.0 mm** pellets. Metsästäjäliitto gives shotgun ranges as "enintään 35 metriä" (at most 35 m).

**EU lead.**

- **REACH wetland ban:** in force since **15 Feb 2023** (Reg. 2021/57). No lead shot (≥ 1 % Pb) may be fired or carried in, or within 100 m of, a wetland.
- **New REACH restriction, Commission Regulation (EU) 2026/2530 of 16 Nov 2026:** published in the Official Journal 17 Nov 2026, in force **7 Dec 2026**. It **bans carrying or firing lead shot while hunting from 7 Dec 2033** and bans placing it on the market from the same date. Point-of-sale warnings start 7 Jun 2027 and packaging labels 7 Jun 2028. **Slugs and bullets are excluded.**

**Lethality science** has two parts: enough energy (penetration) per pellet, and enough pellets on the vitals. The published numbers:

- **Eley (via a hosted copy):** snipe 2 pellets ≥ 0.5 ft-lb (0.68 J); grouse 3 ≥ 0.85 ft-lb (1.15 J); goose 4 ≥ 1.5 ft-lb (2.03 J).
- **Åkerman/Burrard (via shotguns.se):** 1.08 J for small game and 2.16 J for large game; 3, 4 or 5 pellets in the vitals for game of < 0.5 kg, 0.5–1 kg and > 1 kg.
- **German Wannsee norm (hare):** ≥ 5 hits of 3.5 mm shot on 552 cm², about **5.9 J per pellet** (3.5 mm lead at 35 m).
- **Roster (US):** 1–2 hits on waterfowl and 2–3 on pheasant with the right pellet size; minimum pattern counts in a 30-inch circle of 50–145, by species.
- **Danish wounding work:** the campaign for a 25 m maximum on geese **halved wounding of adult pink-footed geese (36 % → 18 %, 1997–2005)**.

**Nordic ethical maximum ranges with shot:**

- **Denmark:** roe deer 20 m, geese and fox 25 m, other game 30 m, minus 5 m for going-away shots, and never a going-away shot at roe deer.
- **Finland:** roe deer 25 m.
- **Sweden:** none official now. The repealed advice was roe deer and geese 20 m.

**Danger range:**

- **Journée's rule:** max range in yards ≈ 2200 × pellet diameter in inches, i.e. about **79 m per mm**.
- **Rule of thumb:** about **100 m per mm**, which leaves a safety margin.
- **Swedish SäkB 2020 (clay shooting):** US 6–9 **250 m**, US 1–5 **300 m**.
- **Slug, SäkB fixed danger area:** **800 m** (1200 m on frozen ground, ice or water).

### 1.3 Which species with shot (hagel) [VERIFIED]

- **3 kap. 13 §:** "Hagelgevär i kaliber 12, 16 och 20 laddade med hagelpatroner får användas vid jakt på samtliga viltarter utom älg, hjortar, visent, bison, myskoxe, mufflonfår, varg, björn, sälar, vildsvin, järv och bäver." *(12/16/20 gauge shotguns loaded with shot may be used on all game except moose, deer [hjortar], wisent, bison, musk ox, mouflon, wolf, bear, seals, wild boar, wolverine and beaver.)*
  - Roe deer (rådjur) are not among "hjortar" in these rules: rådjur are named separately in 2 kap. 13 § and in 3 kap. 16 §. So **rådjur, lodjur (lynx), räv, hare, grävling, gäss etc. may be hunted with 12/16/20 shot**.
  - The old NFS 2002:18, 6 § named "rådjur och lodjur" explicitly, and restricted roe deer with shot to 1 Oct – 31 Jan. That season limit is **not** in NFS 2023:8. [VERIFIED by reading both texts]
- **3 kap. 14 §:** "Hagelgevär i kaliber 24, 28, 32 och .410 laddade med hagelpatroner får endast användas vid jakt på vildkanin, iller, mink, hermelin, ekorre, mullvad, mårdar, vesslor, lämlar, råttor, sorkar (även bisam), möss samt knipa, vigg, gräsand, bläsand, kricka, morkulla, ripa, järpe, fasan, rapphöna, duvor, måsar, trutar, sothöna, skrakar och tättingar (inklusive kråkfåglar)." *(Small gauges only for rabbit, polecat, mink, stoat, squirrel, mole, martens, weasels, lemmings, rats, voles (incl. muskrat), mice, and goldeneye, tufted duck, mallard, wigeon, teal, woodcock, ptarmigan, hazel grouse, pheasant, grey partridge, pigeons/doves, gulls, coot, mergansers and passerines (incl. corvids).)*
  - Not in that list are **hare, fox, geese, black grouse (orre) and capercaillie (tjäder)**. These need 12/16/20.

### 1.4 Pellet size [VERIFIED]

- **3 kap. 12 §:** "Hagelpatron ska vara laddad med hagel vars diameter är 4,1 mm (US nr 1) eller mindre." *(Shot must be 4.1 mm (US no. 1) or smaller.)* This is a **maximum**. **There is no species minimum.**
  - Old NFS 2002:18, 8 § said 4.0 mm (US nr 1).
  - **App note:** steel B, BB and BBB (≈ 4.3–4.8 mm), common in US steel goose loads, are **illegal in Sweden**. Steel US 1 (4.1 mm) is the largest legal steel shot.
- **Exceptions for state-ordered control hunting (skyddsjakt):**
  - **3 kap. 18 §:** wolf or wolverine from a helicopter, and wolverine with a baying dog: "hagelpatronen vara laddad med hagel vars diameter är minst 4,0 mm och högst 8,0 mm" *(pellets of at least 4.0 mm and at most 8.0 mm)*.
  - **3 kap. 19 §:** lynx from a helicopter: "hagel vars diameter är högst 8,0 mm" *(pellets of at most 8.0 mm)*.
- **Tracking wounded game (eftersök), 7 kap. 4 §:** shotguns may be used with "1. slugpatron, eller 2. hagelpatron laddad med hagel vars diameter är 8,0 mm eller mindre på samtliga arter" *(1. a slug, or 2. shot of 8.0 mm or less, on all species)*, "om det behövs för att förkorta viltets lidande eller för att människor och egendom inte ska utsättas för fara" *(when needed to shorten the animal's suffering or to protect people and property)*.

### 1.5 Range rules

- **No legal maximum range with shot or slug in NFS 2023:8.** [VERIFIED: the whole text was read]
- **Repealed (historical) allmänna råd, NFS 2002:18 after 8 §** [VERIFIED, historical]:

  - "Skjutavståndet vid jakt enligt 7 § [slug] bör inte överstiga fyrtio (40) meter." *(With slugs the range should not exceed 40 m.)*

I could not find that recommendation on SvJF's own pages. [UNVERIFIED] (Tobias Hassel, "Alternativhagel för jakt", rev. 2023-11-11, https://www.shotguns.se/Hagelkompendium.pdf)

### 1.6 Slugs (slugpatron) [VERIFIED]

- **3 kap. 16 §:** "Enkelpipigt hagelgevär i kaliber 12, 16 och 20 och med funktionssätt och laddningskapacitet enligt 2–8 §§ får användas med slugpatron *(a single-barrel 12/16/20 gauge shotgun, with action and magazine capacity per §§ 2–8, may be used with slug cartridges)*
  1. vid jakt på vildsvin, dovhjort och mufflonfår av den som är behörig att använda kulgevär avsedda för patroner i klass 1, och *(for wild boar, fallow deer and mouflon, by a person qualified to use a rifle with class 1 cartridges)*
  2. vid jakt på rådjur av den som är behörig att använda kulgevär avsett för patroner i klass 2. *(for roe deer, by a person qualified for class 2 rifles)*

  Vid sådan jakt ska vapnet vara försett med riktmedel som visar vapnets träffpunkt." *(The gun must have sights that show the point of impact.)*
  - **So no slugs on älg (moose) or kronhjort (red deer)** in Sweden, except in eftersök (7 kap. 4 §).
  - **No minimum slug energy or weight.** The kulpatron classes (1 kap. 5 §) are defined for kulpatroner, and "Kulpatron" is defined as rifle ammunition. The slug is a separate category.
- **3 kap. 17 §:** fallow deer may be hunted with a slug at night, with a lamp, night sight or thermal sight, where the county allows hunting around the clock.
- **4 kap. 11 §:** "Kombinationsgevär får inte användas med slugpatron." *(Combination guns may not be used with slugs.)*
- **Rifle classes, for comparison (1 kap. 5 §):**
  - Klass 1: ≥ 9 g and E100 ≥ 2700 J; or ≥ 10 g and E100 ≥ 2000 J; or lead-free ≥ 7.7 g and E100 ≥ 2000 J.
  - Klass 2: ≥ 3.2 g, expanding, E100 ≥ 800 J.
  - Klass 3: ≥ 2.5 g, E100 ≥ 200 J.
  - Klass 4: E0 ≥ 150 J.

### 1.7 Lead shot [VERIFIED unless marked]

**(a) EU REACH wetland restriction, Commission Regulation (EU) 2021/57, Annex XVII entry 63, paragraphs 11–14.** (https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=CELEX:32021R0057)

- **Para 11:** "Doing either of the following acts after 15 February 2023 in or within 100 metres of wetlands is prohibited:"
  - (a) discharging gunshot with a lead concentration "equal to or greater than 1 % by weight";
  - (b) "carrying any such gunshot where this occurs while out wetland shooting or as part of going wetland shooting";
  - (c) a presumption: "the shooting concerned shall be presumed to be wetland shooting" unless the person shows they were doing another type of shooting.
- **Para 13(a):** "wetlands" means "areas of marsh, fen, peatland or water, whether natural or artificial, permanent or temporary…", including marine waters down to 6 m at low tide.
- **Para 12:** a Member State with ≥ 20 % wetland territory could instead ban lead shot nationwide from 15 Feb 2024.
- **Restatement in Swedish law.** NFS 2023:8, 3 kap. 15 § restates the ban: "förbjudet att i eller inom 100 meter från en våtmark a) avlossa hagelammunition med en blyhalt som motsvarar eller överstiger en viktprocent, och b) ha med sig all sådan hagelammunition vid skjutning i våtmarker eller på väg till eller från skjutning i våtmark." *(Prohibited in or within 100 m of a wetland: a) firing shot with ≥ 1 % lead by weight; b) carrying such shot when shooting in wetlands or on the way to or from it.)*
- **Naturvårdsverket's guidance** (page reviewed 9 Sep 2026) adds the EU Court's reading: "Förbudet gäller därför bara i områden med vattenförekomster vars storlek och stabilitet innebär [att de sannolikt fungerar som livsmiljö för vattenfåglar]" *(The ban only applies where the size and stability of the water mean it is likely to be waterbird habitat.)* It also notes that Sweden has had a wetland lead-shot ban since 2002. (https://www.naturvardsverket.se/vagledning-och-stod/jakt-och-vilt/forbud-mot-blyhagel-vid-vatmark/)

**(b) Sweden's own lead rules: förordning (1998:944), as amended by 2023:521** (Riksdagen text "Ändrad t.o.m. SFS 2026:690", https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/forordning-1998944-om-forbud-mm-i-vissa-fall_sfs-1998-944/)

- **14 a §:** "blyhagel: hagelammunition med en blyhalt (uttryckt som metall) som motsvarar eller överstiger 1 viktprocent" *(lead shot means shot with ≥ 1 % lead by weight)*.
- **14 b §:** "Patroner som är laddade med blyhagel får inte användas vid skytte som inte är jakt." *(Lead shot may not be used for shooting that is not hunting.)* In other words, Sweden's national ban covers **sport shooting**, not hunting.
- **14 c §** allows exceptions where there is no acceptable alternative: provskjutning (test firing), jaktstigsskytte (hunting-trail shooting) and jägarexamen tests.
- **NFS 2023:8, 9 kap. 1 §** allows lead for licensed competitors in skeet, trap and double trap.
- **For hunting, Sweden currently has only the EU wetland rule.**

**(c) The EU-wide restriction on lead in hunting gunshot: Commission Regulation (EU) 2026/2530 of 16 November 2026** amending REACH Annex XVII "as regards lead in gunshot". Published in the Official Journal L on 17 Nov 2026; in force 7 Dec 2026 (Article 2: 20th day after publication). (https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=OJ%3AL_202602530) [VERIFIED via EUR-Lex summary pages; the full consolidated annex was not read line by line]

- **Para 28(a):** prohibits "carrying or discharging gunshot while hunting or as part of going hunting, from 7 December 2033", for gunshot with ≥ 1 % lead. Para 28(b) covers outdoor sports shooting from the same date.
- **Para 29:** no placing lead gunshot on the market from 7 Dec 2033.
- **Para 14a:** the 2023 wetland rule (paras 11–14) applies "until 7 December 2033", when the general ban replaces it.
- **Paras 30–31:** a sports-shooting derogation for 1.9–2.6 mm lead shot on ranges with the Appendix 17 risk measures.
- **Para 34:** muzzle-loaders and historic firearms (and modern replicas) are exempt.
- **Paras 32–33, labels:** a point-of-sale warning from **7 June 2027** ("WARNING : this product contains lead which is very toxic to the environment…") and on packaging from **7 June 2028**.
- **Para 38:** Member States may keep stricter national use rules that were in force on 7 Dec 2026.
- **Recitals 29 and 31:** gunshot "should not include slugs", and the Commission found it appropriate "to exclude bullets from the scope". **Lead slugs and lead bullets are not restricted.**
- **How it got here** [SECONDARY]:
  - The Commission's Dec 2025 draft kept 3 years for shot and 5 years for bullets.
  - In spring 2026 shot went to 5 years.
  - The REACH Committee approved a 7-year version without bullets on 25 June 2026.
  - On 8 Oct 2026 the European Parliament rejected objections.
  - (FACE, https://www.face.eu/2026/06/member-states-approve-revised-restriction-on-lead-in-gunshot/; https://www.face.eu/2025/12/updates-on-the-reach-restriction-on-lead-in-ammunition/; club-caza https://www.club-caza.com/en/art/36945; wildbeimwild https://wildbeimwild.com/en/eu-postpones-lead-ammunition-ban-in-hunting-ammunition-for-seven-years/)
- **Finnish confirmation.** Suomen Metsästäjäliitto's release of 17.11.2026 confirms the dates: "Lyijyhauleja saa siten käyttää metsästyksessä 6. joulukuuta 2033 saakka." *(Lead shot may thus be used in hunting until 6 December 2033.)* (https://www.sttinfo.fi/tiedote/72509541/eun-lyijyhaulirajoitus-voimaan-seitseman-vuoden-siirtymaaika-alkaa-joulukuussa?lang=fi)

### 1.8 Steel and other alternatives [VERIFIED unless marked]

There are no legal rules on shot material other than lead. These are the recommendations:

**Svenska Jägareförbundet, "Rätt ammunition" leaflet** (https://qr.jagareforbundet.se/contentassets/7830c70c9d5349f28c583f1dd84fee4c/nytt-korr-ratt-ammunition-a4.pdf):

- Steel "ger därför en lägre anslagsenergi, deformeras inte lika väl som bly, ger högre tryck i vapnet och ger en större risk för rikoschetter … En god regel är att gå ner två US-nummer." *(Steel gives lower striking energy, deforms less, gives higher pressure and more ricochet risk. A good rule is to go two US numbers coarser.)*
- "Vismut … går man ner ett US-nummer … tungsten gäller det omvända. Gå upp två US-nummer" *(Bismuth: one number coarser. Tungsten: the reverse, two numbers finer.)*
- Proof: "patroner med 32 g stålhagel eller däröver, samt patroner med hårda hagel i större grovlek än US 4 ska definitivt bara användas i vapen som av tillverkaren uppges tåla motsvarande hagelblandningar." *(Loads of 32 g steel or more, or hard shot coarser than US 4, only in guns the maker approves for them.)*

**SvJF "Jaktskytte" folder** (link in 1.5): "gå ner två eller tre US-nummer. En vanlig blyhagelpatron för fågeljakt är US 6 … stål-hagel … US 3 till 4 … tungstenspatroner … US 7 till 8." *(Go two or three numbers coarser: lead US 6 becomes steel US 3–4, or tungsten US 7–8.)*

- Choke: "Vid användning av hårda hageltyper som stålhagel eller tungsten ska du aldrig använda trängre choker än halv trångborrning" *(With steel or tungsten never use more than half choke.)*

**Naturvårdsverket, "Jaga blyfritt med hagel"** (https://www.naturvardsverket.se/vagledning-och-stod/jakt-och-vilt/jaga-blyfritt-med-hagel/):

- Pellet size: "gå upp två US-nummer i hagelstorlek, exempelvis från US 5 till US 3" and "hagelstorleken ökas med 0,5 mm (två US-nr)". For bismuth and copper, one number (≈ 0.25 mm) is usually enough.
- Choke: "Det finns vanligtvis ingen anledning att använda mer än halv trångborrningsgrad vid jakt med stålhagel."
- **Roe deer X-ray study cited there:** 60 roe deer shot in normal hunting, X-rayed afterwards. Loads were lead US 4 and steel US 2 / US 4, all 32 g. "Skjutavståndet var i genomsnitt cirka 20 meter, men varierade inom 8-32 meter." *(Average range about 20 m, varying 8–32 m.)* Steel penetrated slightly deeper than lead. [SECONDARY: NV summary. The NV reading list includes Strandgaard 1988, "En undersögelse over jernhagels anvendelighed til jagt på rådyr", Vildtbiologisk station. NV does not say explicitly that this is the study.]
- **UK penetration study** (Champion 2021, Cranfield report CU/CDE/SMC/2231/21, as summarised by NV): 3.2 mm steel and 2.6 mm lead penetrated gelatin covered with pheasant skin and feathers about equally, out to about 50 yd. Without skin, lead went about 10 mm deeper. [SECONDARY]

## 4. Lethality science

### 4.1 The two criteria

A clean kill with shot needs **(1) enough pellets in the vital area** and **(2) each pellet arriving with enough energy, or energy per cross-section, to reach the vitals**. Pattern density fails first with distance; pellet energy fails later.

- Swedish Hassel compendium: "pattern quality usually fails well before pellet energy does". [SECONDARY]
- A summary of Pierce et al. 2014 (doves) says pattern density, not energy per pellet, drove lethality. [UNVERIFIED: only via a search summary of Thomas, Kanstrup & Gremse, Oxford Lead Symposium; the PDF returned 404]
- Roster (2023): "there's a minimum threshold of pellet strikes within a 30-inch circle for lethality". [VERIFIED] (Outdoor Life, Feb 22, 2023, https://www.outdoorlife.com/guns/steel-shot-lethality-testing/)

### 4.5 Nordic wounding (crippling) studies

- **Noer & Madsen 1996** ("Shotgun pellet loads and infliction rates in pink-footed geese", Wildlife Biology 2:65–73): X-rays of live geese caught in W Denmark, spring 1990–92. Pellets were found in **25 % of first-years (n = 69) and 36 % of older geese (n = 286)**. [SECONDARY: abstract via search]
- **Madsen & Noer 1996:** geese carrying pellets had lower annual survival, **0.77 vs 0.87**. [SECONDARY]
- **Noer, Madsen & Hartmann 2007** (J. Appl. Ecol. 44:653–662): "the basic remedial action to reduce wounding has been to encourage compliance with the **25 m maximum range** recommended for shooting geese in Denmark". The background was evidence that "for certain species nearly one bird was wounded for every one killed". [SECONDARY]
  - The Danish action plan (from 1997) "reduced the crippling rate in adult pink-footed geese … from approximately **36% to 18%** between 1997 and 2005 … primarily due to reduced shooting distances". [VERIFIED as quoted in Buij et al. 2026, below]
- **Holm & Haugaard 2013:** embedded pellets in adult female common eiders fell **34 % → 5.5 %**, 1997–2011. [VERIFIED as quoted in Buij et al. 2026]
- **Clausen et al. 2017** (Ecological Indicators 80:242–246, https://pure.au.dk/ws/files/113035534/Clausen_et_al._2017_EcolInd.pdf) [VERIFIED]:
  - "Main causes of crippling are shooting at birds at too long ranges, use of suboptimal shotgun ammunition and shotguns and, more generally, lack of experience". Since 1990 the Danish data are mainly steel shot, and "we have no reason to believe that the ban of lead-based ammunition led to higher levels of crippling."
  - [SECONDARY, as quoted in Buij et al. 2026]: crippling ratio for juveniles fell 1.00 → 0.11 and for adults 9.75 → 1.99 (1992–2016).
- **Buij, Moonen … Liljebäck, Månsson … Madsen 2026** ("Crippling rates of waterfowl by gunshot in Northwestern Europe", Eur. J. Wildl. Res. 72:35, https://edepot.wur.nl/711775) [VERIFIED]:
  - 3,843 waterfowl of 9 species X-rayed in 6 countries (incl. Sweden, Norway, Denmark), 2017–2022; **17 % crippled**.
  - Highest rates: Bewick's swan 33 %, greylag 28%. Lowest: wigeon 5%, brent 4%.
  - Adults 19 % vs first-years 4 %.
  - They recommend guidelines on "hunters' choice of ammunition and shotguns and assessment of shooting ranges at which birds are realistically downed".
- **Sweden, greylag geese** (Månsson, Liljebäck et al. 2024, Wildlife Biology, doi 10.1002/wlb3.01356): 21 % of 176 X-rayed geese carried pellets, about 2 per bird on average, as quoted in a Riksdag question of Jan 2025 (https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/skriftlig-fraga/skadeskjutning_hc11735/). [SECONDARY]
- **Roe deer with shot:** the Danish X-ray study above (60 deer, mean range 20 m). [SECONDARY]

### 4.8 [MY CALC] Expected hits on the vitals

This illustrates the computation the app could do. Pellets in the 30" (76.2 cm, 4560 cm²) circle come from Eley's choke-percentage table in metres (Spencer Table 8). Spread uniformly, the expected number of hits on the vital area is λ = N × p(range, choke) × A_vital / 4560 cm². A uniform spread underestimates a well-centred hit (see centre concentration in 4.3).

**Eley percentage of the load inside the 30" circle** (lead) [SECONDARY]:

| Choke | 20 m | 25 m | 30 m | 35 m | 40 m | 45 m | 50 m |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| True cyl | 75 | 63 | 53 | 43 | 35 | 28 | 22 |
| Imp. cyl. | 85 | 74 | 64 | 53 | 43 | 34 | 27 |
| ¼ | 90 | 80 | 70 | 58 | 48 | 39 | 31 |
| ½ | 97 | 86 | 76 | 64 | 54 | 43 | 34 |
| ¾ | 100 | 93 | 83 | 70 | 58 | 47 | 38 |
| Full | 100 | 100 | 90 | 74 | 62 | 51 | 41 |

**Examples with ½ choke, uniform spread:**

- Roe deer (254 cm²), 32 g lead US 3 (125 pellets): λ ≈ 6.8 at 20 m, 6.0 at 25 m, 5.3 at 30 m, 4.5 at 35 m.
- Roe deer, 32 g steel US 1 (115 pellets): λ ≈ 6.2 at 20 m, 5.5 at 25 m, 4.9 at 30 m.
- Hare/fox (64 cm²), 32 g lead US 5 (200 pellets): λ ≈ 2.7 at 20 m, 2.1 at 30 m.
- Compare the exam's 8 pellets of US 7 in Ø 9 cm, or 28 in Ø 18 cm, at 20 m. A uniform model of 32 g US 7 (345 pellets) at 20 m gives λ ≈ 4.7 and 18.7. **The exam assumes a well-centred, dense core.** The app should model the pattern's centre concentration, ideally calibrated from the hunter's own pattern board.

## 5. Danger range (riskavstånd)

- **Journée's formula:** "if pellet diameter in inches is multiplied by 2,200 then the sum is the theoretical distance of shot travel in yards". Examples: UK No 6 (0.10 in) gives 220 yd; No 5 (0.11 in) gives 242 yd. "A powerful following wind" can add about 33 %. [VERIFIED] (Shooting UK, https://www.shootinguk.co.uk/news/how-far-do-shotgun-pellets-travel/)
  - In SI units: max range (m) ≈ **79 × d (mm)**.
- **The "d (mm) × 100 m" rule.** A German ammunition maker's glossary (RWS, "Overall effective range") gave: "Approximate value in meters: shot diameter in mm x 100", and noted that this is the total flight distance, "not to be mixed up with the long-range limit". Outputs are downrange velocity, energy, "energy density, and gel penetration". It replaces the penetration and downrange parts of Ed Lowry's discontinued "Shotshell Ballistics for Windows". No pattern modelling or graphs per the reviewer. [SECONDARY] A 2025 Australian adaptation (GMA Victoria, "GMA0035 Lethality Table v4") converts it to metres. [UNVERIFIED: PDF not retrieved]

**Lead and steel rules to encode:**

- **EU, until 6 Dec 2033:** lead shot (≥ 1 % Pb) is banned in, or within 100 m of, a wetland. That covers firing and carrying on the way to or from wetland shooting. There is a presumption of wetland shooting.
- **EU, from 7 Dec 2033:** no lead shot for any hunting (carrying or firing). Lead slugs remain legal.
- **FI:** no lead shot for waterfowl, anywhere, since 1996.
- **SE:** no lead shot for non-hunting shooting (with exceptions). For hunting, only the EU rule applies.
- **DK:** no lead shot at all since 1996.
- **Steel guidance:**
  - Two US numbers coarser than lead (+0.5 mm).
  - Bismuth or copper one number coarser; tungsten one or two numbers finer.
  - No more than ½ choke with steel or tungsten.
  - Steel ≥ 32 g, or harder than US 4, only in steel-proofed guns (SvJF).
- **Sweden size limit with steel:** 4.1 mm. Steel B/BB/BBB are illegal.

### 7.2 What the app could compute and show

1. **Pellet ballistics per load:**
   - Inputs: pellet diameter (mm), material density (Pb 11.0–11.3, Bi ≈ 9.6, steel 7.86, tungsten ≈ 12–18), muzzle velocity (V1.5 or V2.5), air density from the existing weather feature.
   - Use a sphere drag curve Cd(Mach). Show velocity, **energy per pellet (J)** and, ideally, energy density (J/mm²) against range.
   - The model in 4.7 matches Eley's table within a few percent. Calibrate against published manufacturer data where available.
2. **Pattern model:**
   - Pellet count from load weight and pellet mass. Note that the Swedish table counts differ slightly from pure-lead calculations.
   - Percentage in the 76 cm circle by choke and range (Eley Table 8 in metres), with a radial density profile calibrated to the 20"/30" ratios (46–62 %).
   - Better: let the hunter **enter a pattern-board count** (pellets in a 76 cm circle at a known distance) and fit the spread from that. This is Roster's method.
   - Steel patterns tighter (about one choke step) is an assumption to be validated, not a sourced number. [UNVERIFIED]
3. **Expected hits on the vitals and the probability of a clean kill:**
   - λ = expected pellets in the vital area, given a well-aimed centre hit and/or an aiming error.
   - P(hits ≥ N_min) from a Poisson model, counting only pellets with E ≥ E_min.
   - Show it as a curve against range, with the threshold where P drops below, say, 90 % (an assumption to choose).
4. **"Ethical range" for shot** = the shorter of:
   - (a) the range where pellet energy drops below the species threshold;
   - (b) the range where the hit probability drops below target;
   - (c) the recommended maximum for that species and country.

   Show which one is binding. Usually (b) or (c) for roe deer and fox; often (a) for small steel on geese.
5. **Legality check** per species and country:
   - shot allowed?
   - gauge allowed (SE small gauges, FI < 20 gauge list)?
   - pellet ≤ 4.1 mm (SE) / ≤ 4 mm (DK)?
   - slug allowed, with which exam qualification (SE klass 1/2)?
   - magazine (SE 2+1, FI ≤ 3, DK/NO 2 total)?
   - lead (wetland / waterfowl / Denmark / after 2033)?
6. **Lead-to-steel converter:** "Your lead US 5 → steel US 3 (+0.5 mm)". Show equal-energy distances, flag the 4.1 mm limit in Sweden, and warn about choke ≤ ½ and steel proof.
7. **Danger range:**
   - Shot: show the SäkB values (US 6–9 250 m, US 1–5 300 m), and/or compute the maximum range from the drag model, with Journée (79 m/mm) and the conservative ×100 m/mm rule for comparison. Add margins for following wind (+33 % per Shooting UK) and for steel ricochet near hard surfaces.
   - Slug: 800 m (1200 m on frozen ground, ice or water) per SäkB, or a computed maximum range.
8. **Slug ballistics:** reuse the existing rifle engine (drag, drop, energy). There is no slug energy class in Sweden or Finland, so show energy only. Show the repealed Swedish 40 m advice as historical, if at all.
9. **Exam reference card:** "Jägarexamen: within range ≤ 20 m; 8 pellets on Ø 9 cm (hare/fox), 28 on Ø 18 cm (roe deer)". Compare with what the user's load gives.

### 7.3 Caveats for the app text

- Swedish and Finnish law set **no minimum pellet size and no maximum shotgun range**. Ranges and sizes shown are **recommendations**; label them with the organisation that gives them.
- Pellet "US" numbers differ between the Swedish/European, true US and English scales. Show mm.
- The energy thresholds in 4.2 come from old British empirical work (Eley/Burrard), German test norms (Wannsee) and US penetration work (Lowry). None is a Nordic regulation. Present them as model assumptions with sources.
- Gaps still to fill: vital areas for geese, fox (whole body), grouse and capercaillie; a primary source for Journée/Burrard; the ×100 rule in a Swedish source; Finnish danger ranges.
